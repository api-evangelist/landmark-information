Kellen Homes is a new property developer based in Warrington, operating in Greater Manchester. They specialise in timber-framed houses, bespoke apartments, and storage units. This case study explores how Kellen Homes utilises Landmark Geodata’s BiodiversityCheck product to streamline their Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) assessments during the land acquisition process.
